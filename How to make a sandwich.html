VEGETABLE CHEESE SANDWICH RECIPE

Ingredients:

Base

  • Bread slices – 4 (white, brown, or multigrain as you like)
  • Butter – 2 tablespoons (soft, at room temperature)

Spread (optional but adds taste)

  • Green chutney (mint-coriander chutney) – 2 tablespoons (or use mayonnaise or tomato ketchup if you prefer)

Vegetable filling

  • Onion – 1 small (thinly sliced)
  • Tomato – 1 small (thinly sliced)
  • Cucumber – 1 small (thinly sliced)
  • Capsicum (bell pepper) – ¼ cup (thinly sliced)
  • Boiled potato – 1 small (optional, thin slices)
  • Grated carrot – 2 tablespoons (optional)
  • Lettuce or spinach leaves – few (optional for freshness)

Cheese

  • Cheese slice – 2 (or grated mozzarella/cheddar as per your taste)

Seasoning

  • Salt – a pinch
  • Black pepper powder – ¼ teaspoon
  • Chaat masala – ¼ teaspoon (optional for Indian flavor)

Preparation Steps

STEP:1 Prepare the vegetables

  1. Wash all vegetables thoroughly.
  2. Peel and slice onion, cucumber, and tomato thinly.
  3. If using potatoes, boil them until soft and slice them thinly.
  4. Keep all the veggies ready on a plate.

STEP 2: Prepare the spread

  1. Take butter in a bowl. If you like, mix a bit of chutney or ketchup with butter for flavor.
  2. Alternatively, apply green chutney on one slice and butter or mayo on the other.

STEP 3: Assemble the sandwich

  1. Take two bread slices.
  2. Apply butter (or chutney/mayo) evenly on both slices.
  3. Place the sliced vegetables one by one — cucumber, tomato, onion, capsicum, potato, carrot.
  4. Sprinkle a pinch of salt, pepper, and chaat masala over them
  5. Add a cheese slice (or grated cheese).
  6. Cover it with the second bread slice.

STEP 4: Toast or Grill

    Option 1: Plain (no heat)

  • Just cut it diagonally and serve cold.
  • Perfect for a light snack

    • Option 2: Toasted

  • Apply butter on the outer sides of both bread slices.
  • Place the sandwich on a hot tawa/pan and toast on both sides till golden brown.

    • Option 3: Grilled

  • Use a sandwich maker or grill pan.
  • Grill until the cheese melts and bread turns crispy.

STEP 5: Serve

  • Cut diagonally or in halves.
  • Serve hot with ketchup, chutney, or a cold drink.
